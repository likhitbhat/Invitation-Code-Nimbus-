# Wedding Invitation Home Page Recreation (`wed002`)

Exact 1-to-1 recreation of the home page of [https://wed002-livedemo.invitationnation.in/](https://wed002-livedemo.invitationnation.in/) including all elements, fonts, high-resolution graphics, animations, countdown timers, interactive cards, and audio controls.

---

## 🌸 Features & Sections Recreated

1. **Floating Navigation Header**:
   - Clean top bar with tabs: `Home`, `Story`, `Gallery`.
2. **Hero Cover Screen**:
   - Swinging vintage lanterns on left and right corners.
   - Lord Ganesha blessing icon and script calligraphy *"Save the Date"*.
   - Floral flourishes and couple names: **Aryan kapoor & Aarna Nair**.
   - Wedding date badge: **NOV | 26 & 27 | 2026**.
   - Venue location label: **Fiestaa Resort**.
   - Interactive pill button: **Open in Maps** (linking to Google Maps).
   - Taj Mahal backdrop silhouette with soft pink lotus frame.
   - Caricature illustration of the bride and groom holding hands.
3. **Live Countdown Timer 1**:
   - Title: *"Officially Husband and Wife in"*.
   - Real-time ticking down timer for **Days, Hours, Minutes, and Seconds**.
4. **Bride Bio Card**:
   - Traditional Indian palace arch frame cutout with Aarna Nair's portrait.
   - Details: **Aarna Nair**, *D/o Mr. Suresh Nair & Mrs. Lakshmi Nair*.
   - Personalized bio text.
5. **Groom Bio Card**:
   - Traditional Indian palace arch frame cutout with Aryan Kapoor's portrait.
   - Details: **Aryan kapoor**, *S/o Mr. Rajesh Kapoor & Mrs. Meena Kapoor*.
   - Personalized bio text and *"More About The Couple"* button.
6. **Events & Functions Carousel ("Join the celebration on")**:
   - Left pane with headline, slide indicator dots, and previous/next interactive arrow buttons.
   - Mughal arch card with mandala floral corner borders and Taj Mahal watermark.
   - Event details: **Thursday, 26 November 2026**, **Haldi** at **7:30 pm**, **Fiestaa Resort**, with **Open in Maps** action.
7. **"Send your wishes" Interactive Card**:
   - Layered pink container flanked by lotus flowers and lily pads.
   - Text inputs for *Your Name* and *Your Wishes* with AI/sparkle icon and *Submit* button.
8. **Live Countdown Timer 2 & Couple Quote**:
   - Title: *"Awaiting the Big Day with Open Hearts"*.
   - Center caricature of the couple flanked by live countdown numbers.
9. **Footer**:
   - Official branding: *"Wedding Invitation website by INVITATIONNATION"*.
   - Links: *Report a Problem*, *Contact Support*, *Privacy Policy*.
   - Copyright notices and credits.
10. **Floating Action Controls**:
    - Call button (dials contact number).
    - Floating music player button (toggles audio playback on/off).

## 🚀 Running Locally

To run the local server:

```bash
npm start
```

Then open your browser at:
```
http://localhost:8080/
```
*(All fonts, SVGs, high-resolution caricatures, photos, styles, and scripts run self-contained locally).*
